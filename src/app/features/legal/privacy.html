Chili Platform

Privacy policy

This policy describes what Chili Platform stores about an account, a game, a forum post, a checkout, and a creator payout. It is written to match the service as it runs today. Account rules are in the terms of service. Marketplace sales are in the seller terms.

Account email

Registration stores your email address. It is unique on the service. Chili uses it to sign you in, to send the verification link, and to send a password reset link when one is requested. The public profile does not show the email address.

Profile

The profile stores your username, a short bio, and an avatar URL when you set one. The public profile shows the username, the bio, the avatar URL, and when the account was created.

Games and covers

Game projects, including the Bitsy file, are stored in Cloudflare D1. Cover images are PNG files stored in Cloudflare R2 and served from the site's /media/ path. A released game's title, owner, and cover are public. The game file is returned to the owner and to someone who has the game in their library.

Forum posts

Forum posts and comments are stored in Cloudflare D1. They are readable without an account. Each one is tied to the username that wrote it.

Stripe customer id

When checkout creates a Stripe customer for your account, Chili stores that Stripe customer id on the account. It is an identifier Stripe assigns. It is not your card number.

Checkout

Game purchases and kit orders are paid through Stripe Checkout. Stripe collects the card details. Chili does not store the card number.

A game purchase uses the email on your account and the listed price. Chili records the purchase, the price, and the payment status.

A kit order also asks Stripe Checkout for a shipping address. Chili stores that address and the email on the order so the kit can be sent. Kit sales are Chili's own sales. They are separate from creator payouts.

Connect identity

Creator payout setup uses Stripe Connect inside the Chili sales page. Stripe collects identity and bank details there. Chili's own forms do not collect that information. Chili stores the connected account id Stripe assigns, and the transfer and payout status Stripe reports.

Cloudflare request logs

The API runs on Cloudflare Workers with observability turned on at a full sample rate. Cloudflare therefore keeps request logs for the API. Those logs can include the request path, timing, and the network data Cloudflare records for a Worker request.

What stays in the browser

After you sign in, the site stores the access token and the refresh token in the browser's local storage so later visits stay signed in. Signing out deletes them from that browser.

Who else receives it

Stripe receives what Checkout and Connect need to take a payment or pay a creator. Cloudflare receives the request data described above because it hosts the API, the database, the file bucket, and the site. Chili uses those companies to run the service.
