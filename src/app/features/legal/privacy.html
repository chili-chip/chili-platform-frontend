Privacy policy

Legal bases (EU and Poland)

chilichip processes personal data under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Polish data protection law. Depending on what you do on the site, chilichip relies on:

  • Contract — to run your account, save games, run the forum, take payments, deliver digital games, ship kits, and pay creators.
  • Legal obligation — to keep records chilichip must keep for tax, accounting, or payment rules.
  • Legitimate interests — to secure the service, prevent abuse, and keep API request logs, balanced against your rights.
  • Consent — only where chilichip asks for it separately (for example accepting updated policies where the law requires consent). Creating an account means accepting the terms and this policy, as described in the terms of service.

Minors

If you are not old enough to enter a contract where you live, you may use chilichip Platform only with the permission of a parent or legal guardian. In Poland, consent to information society services without a parent is generally possible from age 16. A parent or legal guardian may write to chilichipelectronics@gmail.com with questions about a minor's account or the information chilichip stores about that account.

Account email

Registration stores your email address. It is unique on the service. chilichip uses it to sign you in, to send the verification link, and to send a password reset link when one is requested. The public profile does not show the email address.

Profile

The profile stores your username, a short bio, and an avatar URL when you set one. The public profile shows the username, the bio, the avatar URL, and when the account was created.

Games and covers

Game projects, including the Bitsy file, are stored in Cloudflare D1. Cover images are PNG files stored in Cloudflare R2 and served from the site's /media/ path. A released game's title, owner, and cover are public. The game file is returned to the owner and to someone who has the game in their library.

Forum posts

Forum posts and comments are stored in Cloudflare D1. They are readable without an account. Each one is tied to the username that wrote it.

Stripe customer id

When checkout creates a Stripe customer for your account, chilichip stores that Stripe customer id on the account. It is an identifier Stripe assigns. It is not your card number.

Checkout

Game purchases and kit orders are paid through Stripe Checkout. Stripe collects the card details. chilichip does not store the card number.

A game purchase uses the email on your account and the listed price. chilichip records the purchase, the price, and the payment status.

A kit order also asks Stripe Checkout for a shipping address. chilichip stores that address and the email on the order so the kit can be sent. Kit sales are chilichip's own sales. They are separate from creator payouts.

Connect identity

Creator payout setup uses Stripe Connect inside the chilichip sales page. Stripe collects identity and bank details there. chilichip's own forms do not collect that information. chilichip stores the connected account id Stripe assigns, and the transfer and payout status Stripe reports.

Cloudflare request logs

The API runs on Cloudflare Workers with observability turned on at a full sample rate. Cloudflare therefore keeps request logs for the API. Those logs can include the request path, timing, and the network data Cloudflare records for a Worker request.

What stays in the browser

After you sign in, the site stores the access token and the refresh token in the browser's local storage so later visits stay signed in. Signing out deletes them from that browser. chilichip does not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics on the main site.

Who else receives it

Stripe receives what Checkout and Connect need to take a payment or pay a creator. Cloudflare receives the request data described above because it hosts the API, the database, the file bucket, and the site. chilichip uses those companies as processors under chilichip's instructions. Their privacy notices explain what they keep: Stripe, Cloudflare.

chilichip does not sell personal data and does not share it with advertisers. chilichip may disclose data if the law requires it, or to protect rights, safety, and the integrity of the service.

Transfers outside the EU

Stripe and Cloudflare may process data in the United States and other countries. Where GDPR requires safeguards, chilichip relies on the providers' standard contractual clauses and other transfer tools they offer.

How long it is kept

chilichip keeps data for as long as needed for the purposes above, then deletes or anonymises it unless the law requires longer storage.

  • Account and profile — until you ask chilichip to delete the account, plus a short period for backups and abuse investigations.
  • Games and covers — while the account exists and the game is stored; released public metadata may be removed when you delete the game or close the account, subject to library copies buyers already received.
  • Forum posts and comments — until you delete them or ask chilichip to remove them; chilichip may keep minimal logs if a post was involved in a legal claim.
  • Purchases, orders, and payout records — for the period Polish tax and accounting law requires (often up to five years from the end of the year they relate to).
  • API logs at Cloudflare — for the retention period Cloudflare applies to Workers observability for this project.

Your rights

Under GDPR you may ask chilichip for access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or to object to processing based on legitimate interests. You may withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, without affecting earlier processing. You may lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Poland that is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO), uodo.gov.pl.

Email chilichipelectronics@gmail.com from the address on your account (or explain why you cannot). chilichip will respond within one month, unless the law allows an extension for complex requests. chilichip may ask you to confirm your identity. Some data chilichip must keep even after a deletion request, for example purchase records for tax. chilichip will explain if that applies.

There is no automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects on you.

Changes to this policy

chilichip may update this policy when the service or the law changes. chilichip will post the new text on this page with a new effective date. If a change needs your consent under GDPR, chilichip will ask for it before the change applies to you.
