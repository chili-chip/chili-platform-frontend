Marketplace

Seller terms

These terms apply when you list a game on the Chili Platform marketplace or set up creator payouts. The terms of service still cover your account, the forum, and the creator. The privacy policy describes what is stored.

The operator is [OPERATOR LEGAL NAME], [OPERATOR POSTAL ADDRESS] ("Chili"). Notices go to [LEGAL CONTACT EMAIL]. These terms are effective on [EFFECTIVE DATE].

Who may sell

You may list a game or set up payouts only if you are able to enter a contract. These terms do not add an age gate.

Listing a game

You list a game you released and that you own. Releasing a project does not list it. You may list it for free, or at a price of at least $1. A paid price under $1 is not accepted. A free listing does not charge a card and does not create creator earnings.

You accept these seller terms before the first listing, and before payout setup.

The charge stays with Chili

The buyer pays the listed price by card. That charge is on Chili's account. Chili's name is on it. Chili keeps the charge. Chili handles refunds and disputes. You are not paid at the moment of the sale. You cannot run the refund or the dispute yourself.

A dispute debits Chili. If Chili already transferred your credit, Chili may reverse that transfer. A negative balance from that reversal is Chili's liability.

What Chili keeps

From a paid sale, Chili keeps 20% of the game price, plus an estimate of the card processing fee for that charge. The operator sets the estimate. These terms do not state a card rate. There is no fixed Stripe percentage in this document or in the checkout math.

The estimate is not the fee Stripe actually charges. Actual fees vary by region, card, and payment method. The 20% is the margin Chili intends to keep, not a guaranteed profit. Chili watches the difference between the estimate and the fee Stripe charges.

Your credit is the listed price minus 20% minus that estimate. Chili pays that credit later, by transfer, which is less than the charge. If the 20% and the estimate are greater than the price, your credit for that sale is zero. Chili does not charge you a subscription.

When you are paid

Earnings wait 7 days. After that hold, Chili transfers cleared earnings once they are at least $20, and only after payout setup is finished and Stripe reports that transfers and payouts are active. A cleared balance under $20 stays until later sales make up the difference.

Sales still count if you have not finished payout setup. The balance waits. One payout setup covers later sales. Chili checks that transfers are active before each payout and does not pay while they are not.

Payout setup

Stripe collects identity and bank details inside the Chili sales page. Chili's own forms do not collect that. You do not get a Stripe login. Chili stores the connected account id and the transfer and payout status. The sales page shows your balance, your sales, and that status.

Kit sales

Hardware kit checkout is Chili's own sale. It uses the store, not a creator listing. A kit sale does not create creator earnings and is not part of a creator payout.
